I am a PhD student studying Modern Jewish History in the Department of Near Eastern & Judaic Studies at Brandeis University. I am interested in modern Jewish thought, the intellectual and political history of Zionism and Jewish nationalism, digital humanities, and 19th–20th century debates related to the liberal and nationalist state in Europe.

I am writing my dissertation about a social network of pre-state Zionists who blur the theoretical boundaries between cultural and political Zionism, Hebraism and Yiddishism, and diasporic continuity and settlement in mandatory Palestine. My dissertation asks whether this social network sought to establish Jewish politics in Palestine as part of a larger Jewish diaspora polity — as opposed to a Jewish nation-state, or an imperial province/metropole.
